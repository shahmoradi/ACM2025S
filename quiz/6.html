by Amir Shahmoradi — 22 Apr 2025


Here is the solution to this quiz.


You have 50 minutes to respond to the questions and submit your answers.


  1. (score : 5) What is the strict definition of oscillatory motion in Classical Mechanics?

  2. (score : 15) Consider the following equations of motion.
    \(\begin{eqnarray} \ddot x + \omega^2 x &=& 0. \\ \ddot x + 2\beta\dot x + \omega_0^2 x &=& 0. \\ \ddot x + 2\beta\dot x + \omega_0^2 x &=& A\cos(\omega t). \\ \end{eqnarray}\)
    1. (score : 6) What kind of motion do each of the following differential equations describe?
    2. (score : 6) What kind of contributions do each of the terms in the above equations make to the motion?
    3. (score : 3) Why is there commonly a factor of $2$ associated with the term $\beta\dot x$?

  3. (score : 20) Under what conditions a pendulum behaves like a simple harmonic oscillator? (score : )
    Derive the relevant equation of motion. (score : )

  4. (score : 15) Consider the motion $x(t) = A\sin(\omega t - \delta)$.
    1. (score : 3) What is the physical meaning of $A$ in the position equation above?
    2. (score : 6) Show that this position equation satisfies the simple harmonic oscillator’s equation of motion.
    3. (score : 6) Show that the phase path equation of a one-dimensional simple harmonic oscillator is an ellipse whose semi-major and semi-minor axes are $A$ and $A\omega$.

  5. (score : 15) Consider the motion of a particle described by $x(t) = A \exp(-\frac{\beta}{2}t) \sin(\omega t - \delta)$.
    1. (score : 3) What kind of motion is this?
    2. (score : 6) Derive an equation for the kinetic energy of this system.
    3. (score : 6) Draw a schematic phase diagram for this system.

  6. (score : 30) Consider a pendulum made of a spring with a mass 𝑚 on the end. The spring is arranged to lie in a straight line (which we can arrange by, say, wrapping the spring around a rigid massless rod). The equilibrium length of the spring is $l$. Let the spring have length $l+x(t)$, and let its angle with the vertical be $\theta(t)$. Assuming that the motion takes place in a vertical plane, find the equations of motion for $x$ and $\theta$.
